Hardware findings and committed fixes

f521e03 adds free heap, largest allocation and fragmentation startup diagnostics.
80a5e33 corrects TLS gating to include the configured IRAM heap and the actual
16 KB receive buffer plus 325-byte BearSSL overhead.
0aaa2db additionally reserves a sufficiently large DRAM block for the core's
mandatory 6.2 KB crypto stack and client construction.
822eae2 reports available SSL error details alongside failed HTTPS requests.
3a13d50 added temporary DNS/TCP/handshake distinctions. Their absence on-device
helped identify that HTTPClient never reached the context's connect method.
6dc58a7 fixes that integration bug: HTTPClient calls clone(), but the raw BearSSL
context returns nullptr. The public WiFiClientSecure wrapper supports cloning
and shares its TLS context. Cancellation accesses only the owned context's TCP
socket, preserving TLS state while the cooperative feed worker is suspended.
The temporary connect override was removed by this fix.
