Palette design

Ten dark and four light options, available independently for day and night:
Aurora, Sunset, Terminal, Nord, Dracula, Mocha, Latte, Solarized Dark,
Solarized Light, Paper, Rose, Ocean, Lavender, Graphite.

Material 3 palettes use Material Color Utilities 0.4.0 with documented seeds.
Other palettes adapt the official Nord, Dracula, Catppuccin and Solarized colors.
Source URLs, original adjusted tones and ten role colors are recorded in
src/device-themes.json. Selected tones are adapted for LCD contrast; these are
adaptations, rather than claims of exact unmodified upstream colors.

The build generates both firmware palettes and browser swatches from that
catalog. Swatches reflect the actual RGB565-expanded screen values. Original
IDs 0/1/2 remain compatible with saved settings; their colors are refined.
Both profiles retain their custom color values when choosing a preset.
Custom colors keep all four explicitly chosen roles. Derived muted, positive,
negative and warning roles adapt to the background; the result is cached until
its configuration changes, avoiding repeated floating-point work during redraws.
